# Aesthetic Taste Analysis — Rotary Club of Nairobi South Poster Deck

**Artifact:** Canva design `DAFj1rOFszE` ("rotary nairobi south") — 13 pages, 1080×1080, full Wednesday-6.30PM season of club posters.
**Method:** Pixel-histogram forensics + macOS Vision OCR with bounding boxes (95+ text lines, conf=1.00), mapped against the six-dimension framework.
**Purpose:** Companion doctrine to `../SKILL.md`. This file is the *why*; the SKILL is the *how*.

---

## The Aesthetic Thesis

Institutional ceremonialism compressed into a square. The design surrenders a third of its canvas to identity before it says a single word — then announces everything in monumental condensed capitals, as if the square meter of a phone screen were the façade of a town hall. Authority is inherited ("Since 1963"), persuasion is achieved through scale alone, and warmth lives exclusively in the photography. This is the visual language of Nairobi's hotel-ballroom event economy: civic, welcoming, unambiguously official.

## The Deepest Structural Fact: Invariants vs Variables

Read all 13 pages as one system and a clean separation emerges — the taste is not a look, it is a *constitution* with fixed articles and scheduled amendments:

**Invariants (never vary across any page):**
- Masthead presence, order, and top-band position (~30% of canvas)
- Footer liturgy: WHEN → TIME → WHERE → CONTACT
- Three-band horizontal structure (identity / proclamation / logistics)
- Case discipline: caps above, sentence case below
- Blue `#0060b0` + gold `#f0b000` as the only inherited hues

**Variables (scheduled freedom, one per poster):**
- The photograph (topic-appropriate, always scrimmed)
- One novelty accent hue (magenta game night, teal philosophy)
- Headline subject (never headline *grammar* — always 2 stacked condensed caps lines)
- Venue/contact strings inside the fixed footer slots

This is why the deck reads as a series rather than as thirteen flyers: the eye learns the grammar once and thereafter reads only the variables. **Designing one poster in this system means editing variables; touching an invariant is a constitutional crisis.**

## Six-Dimension Read

### 1. Information Density & Spatial Economy
Negative space is ceremonial, not contemplative. The top band's emptiness says "this is official"; the hero's density says "attend". Zoned, not uniform: sparse masthead (authority needs air), high-ink hero (persuasion needs mass), compressed footer (logistics need order). Padding conveys hierarchy vertically — the amount of air above an element measures its rank.

### 2. Structural Composition & Scaffolding
A rigid tripartite grid filtered through corporate identity guidelines — closer to a Swiss municipal poster than to editorial flow. Hierarchy is explicit and heavy-handed: nothing is discovered, everything is announced. Boundaries are pure spatial juxtaposition; no hairlines, cards, or elevation. The structure must survive with every border removed — and it does, because type scale *is* the border.

### 3. Restraint, Tension & Craft
Restraint: palette constitution, case discipline, two typefaces max. Indulgence: the photography, where all sentiment is permitted (under a scrim). The productive tension is scale contrast — monumental condensed caps against micro-logistics, proclamation against whisper. The deck's failures are precisely where tension tips into damage: ~18px fine print converts "whisper" into "illegible", and overlapping fragments ("TAconcert") break the one-textbox-per-idea rule. The system is the taste; lapses are execution, not doctrine.

### 4. Tone, Stance & Cultural Lineage
Civic-institutional warmth. Lineage: mid-century civic modernism (Rotary's 1919-era identity) × East African corporate print culture (hotel-ballroom economy; an M-PESA till number is a legitimate design element) × NGO poster vernacular (stacked caps over scrimmed photography). The implied audience is Nairobi's professional class — people who read "Since 1963" and "Hotel Boulevard" as establishment signals and a WhatsApp number as an open door.

### 5. Motion, State & Tactile Dynamics
A static medium, but the scrim behaves like a state layer: photo = feeling ON, scrim = legibility ON — two states, one element. Depth is flat by conviction: hierarchy is planar (size + position only), shadows would be a lie the system refuses to tell.

## Core Design Principles

1. **Identity is infrastructure, not decoration.** The masthead is load-bearing; remove it and the poster collapses into an anonymous flyer. Never shrink it to make room.
2. **Typography is the architecture.** Type scale alone separates the three bands (8:4:3:2). Structure that needs borders was never structure.
3. **Emotion lives in the photograph, never in the type.** Type stays declarative; the scrimmed photo carries all sentiment.
4. **Logistics follow a liturgy.** WHEN → TIME → WHERE → CONTACT, fixed order, fixed phrasing ("6.30PM"). Predictability *is* the hospitality.
5. **The palette is inherited, not chosen.** Blue and gold are the constitution; one novelty accent per poster is the only permitted amendment.

## Taste Profile & Sensory Signature

- **Density:** Balanced with progressive zoning — ceremonial emptiness above, high ink in the middle, compressed utility below.
- **Temperament:** Civic-ceremonial — Utilitarian logistics with a deliberately Luxurious spatial tax paid to identity. Never Clinical (warm photos), never Expressive (type does not perform).
- **Lineage:** Mid-century civic modernism × East African corporate print culture × NGO poster vernacular.
- **Fidelity & Finish:** Engineered system, uneven execution — the grammar is disciplined even where individual pages lapsed.

## Actionable Taste Matrix

| Observed Mechanism | Aesthetic Intention | Design Directive |
|---|---|---|
| Top-third masthead band, fixed | Heritage-as-authority, borrowed from banks and law firms | Reserve top ~30% for identity on every page; content never invades |
| Giant condensed ALL-CAPS, max two stacked lines | The square is a town-hall façade; the headline is a proclamation | Headline = middle third, 2 lines max, 8:4:3:2 ratio |
| Dark navy scrim over photography | Photography supplies warmth; scrim guarantees legibility | Never type on unscrimmed imagery; scrim ≈ 60–75% navy |
| Fixed footer liturgy | A regular finds the date blind | WHEN→TIME→WHERE→CONTACT, same zone, same phrasing |
| Blue/gold duotone + one novelty accent | Constitution with one scheduled amendment | Two inherited hues + one mood accent; a second accent is a reject |
| Square 1080×1080 | The feed is the venue | Design square-first; every crop downstream is a derivative |
| Case boundary = hierarchy boundary | Authority above, conversation below | Never mix cases within a zone |
| One focal event per page | One poster, one ask | Split multi-event content into pages; a page that asks twice asks zero times |

## What This Design Explicitly Rejects

- Gradient meshes and SaaS purple-blue washes
- Drop shadows, elevation planes, any depth simulation
- Cards, rounded rectangles, pill containers
- Hairline rules and divider lines as structure
- Glassmorphism and frosted overlays (a scrim is not a blur)
- More than two typefaces per page
- Lowercase or mixed-case headlines
- Decorative icons, clip-art, illustration collages
- Photography without a legibility scrim
- Bullet-point soup in the hero zone — the hero holds a proclamation, not a syllabus
- Reordering or rewording the logistics liturgy
- A second novelty accent alongside the first
- Palette drift between pages of one deck
- Fine print below ~2% of canvas height (the deck itself sinned here)
- Overlapping text fragments ("TAconcert" shipped once; never again)
- Hype punctuation — confidence is typographic, not exclamatory
- Broken-grid editorial layouts — the bands are horizontal and honest
- Treating the masthead as negotiable canvas space
- Varying an invariant to "freshen" a page — freshness lives only in the variables
